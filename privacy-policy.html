Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026 | Governing Entity: properties-eg.co

1. Corporate Entity & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the practices of properties-eg.co ("we," "our," or "the Brokerage"), an authorized real estate brokerage and strategic partner operating within the Arab Republic of Egypt. With over 10 years of market expertise, we are committed to upholding the highest standards of discretion, confidentiality, and data integrity.

This policy outlines how personal information submitted through our digital platforms, lead capture forms, and direct communication channels is collected, processed, and safeguarded.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

Data is collected by properties-eg.co solely for real estate consultation purposes, personalized investment advisory, and matching prospective buyers and investors with premier residential and commercial properties in Egypt.

Personal data collected may include:

  • Full Legal Name: To address you formally and establish consultation records.
  • Phone Number & International Dialing Code: To coordinate private consultation calls, schedule VIP site previews, and provide requested inventory details via phone or authorized encrypted messaging channels (e.g., WhatsApp).
  • Investment Interests & Inquiries: Unit preferences, preferred locations, budget tiers, and timing parameters.
  • Technical Interaction Data: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic parameters, referral sources, and timestamp data.

3. Strict Data Sharing Protocol with Authorized Developers

Under our stringent privacy framework, personal data is shared strictly with authorized developers representing the specific property portfolios or developments you inquire about (such as Emaar Misr, SODIC, Palm Hills Developments, Talaat Moustafa Group, ORA Developers, Mountain View, La Vista Developments, and Qatari Diar).

Zero Third-Party Commercial Resale: properties-eg.co does not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal data to independent telemarketers, advertising networks, or unauthorized third parties under any circumstances.

4. Data Security & Retention

We implement administrative, technical, and physical security measures to shield your data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Data collected is retained strictly for the duration necessary to satisfy real estate advisory requirements and comply with applicable statutory obligations.

You retain the unconditional right to access, rectify, or request the deletion of your personal records from our brokerage database at any time. To exercise these rights or modify your communication preferences, you may contact our compliance desk:
